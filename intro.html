Ages — number + sai (kanji numbers!)
AgeRomajiKanjiAgeRomajiKanji
Add -sai (才/歳) = "years old" — e.g. juuni-sai (十二さい) = 12 years old.
The four patterns
Colour key: topic · wa / ni particle · your info · desu / sunde imasu verb
① Name
Watashi wa [name] desu.  =  I am [name].
▸ Watashi wa Emi desu. = I am Emi.
▸ Ask: Onamae wa? = What's your name?
② Age
[number]-sai desu.  =  I am [number] years old.
▸ Juusan-sai desu. (十三さいです) = I am 13 years old.
▸ Ask: Nansai desu ka? = How old are you?
③ Nationality — country + jin
[country]-jin desu.  =  I am [nationality].
▸ Oosutoraria-jin desu. = I am Australian.
▸ Nihon-jin = Japanese
▸ Amerika-jin = American
▸ Chuugoku-jin = Chinese
④ Where you live
[place] ni sunde imasu.  =  I live in [place].
▸ Shidonii ni sunde imasu. = I live in Sydney.
▸ Ask: Doko ni sunde imasu ka? = Where do you live?
