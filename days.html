Activities (… o shimasu)
Key sentence patterns
▸ [activity] o shimasu = I do / play [activity].
▸ [day] ni [activity] o shimasu = I do [activity] on [day].
▸ shimasu = do / play  ·  shimasen = do NOT do / play (negative).
▸ Nanyoubi ni … o shimasu ka? = What day do you do …?
ni (に) = "on" after the day · o (を) = object particle after the activity.
e.g. Getsuyoubi ni yakyuu o shimasu = I play baseball on Monday · Nichiyoubi ni sukii o shimasen = I don't ski on Sunday.
