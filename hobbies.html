Sentence patterns
Colour key: topic the thing · wa topic marker · B the word · desu / shimasu verb · o object
① Asking
Shumi wa nan desu ka?  =  What is your hobby?
② Answering — A wa B desu
Shumi wa [hobby] desu  =  My hobby is [hobby].
▸ Watashi no / Boku no = "my" — you can add it at the front (or leave it out).
▸ Watashi no shumi wa dansu desu. = My hobby is dance.
③ Doing an activity — ~ o shimasu
[activity] o shimasu  =  I play / do [activity].
▸ Sakkaa o shimasu. = I play soccer.
▸ Ask: Nani o shimasu ka? = What do you play / do?
