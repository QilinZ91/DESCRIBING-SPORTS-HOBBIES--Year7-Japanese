SECTION 2 · Likes and dislikes
Sentence patterns
[sport] ga suki desu.  =  I like [sport].
[sport] ga suki ja nai desu.  =  I don't like [sport].
[sport] wa amari suki ja nai desu.  =  I don't really like [sport].
[sport] ga kirai desu.  =  I really dislike [sport].
[sport] ga suki desu ka?  =  Do you like [sport]?

🔑 THE MAGIC PATTERN · A wa B desu
A wa B desu  =  A is / are B
A は B です
★ Sakkaa wa sugoi desu. → Soccer is great. サッカーは すごいです。
★ Tenisu wa tanoshii desu. → Tennis is fun. テニスは たのしいです。
📚 WORD BANK
🏀 Sports & hobbies: supootsu = sports (スポーツ) · geemu = games (ゲーム) · sakkaa = soccer (サッカー) · tenisu = tennis (テニス) · suiei = swimming (すいえい) · yakyuu = baseball (やきゅう) · ragubii = rugby (ラグビー) · basukettobooru = basketball (バスケットボール) · pinpon = table tennis (ピンポン) · dansu = dance (ダンス) · karate = karate (からて) · sumou = sumo (すもう)
⭐ Adjectives: sugoi = great (すごい) · tanoshii = fun (たのしい) · tsumaranai = boring (つまらない) · omoshiroi = interesting (おもしろい) · muzukashii = difficult (むずかしい) · yasashii = easy (やさしい)
